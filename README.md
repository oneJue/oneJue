## About

I am a master's student at the **School of Data Science and Engineering, East China Normal University (ECNU)**. I am a member of the **Decision Intelligence Lab**, advised by Prof. **Bin Yang**.

My work focuses on time series analysis and deep learning, with an emphasis on building and evaluating general-purpose time series models.

## Research Interests

- Time series foundation and large models
- Multimodal time series modeling
- Time series benchmarking and evaluation
